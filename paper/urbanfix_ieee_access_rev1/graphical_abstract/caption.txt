UrbanFix takes a civic complaint from the citizen's photo to a verified public record: (1) the citizen reports without an account, (2) a per-category YOLOv8n model screens the photo and flags the complaint for review when its confidence is below a threshold chosen on validation data, never rejecting it, (3) a supervisor triages it with a traceable questionnaire urgency, and (4) the closed record is published without the filer's identity to a public registry, and approved researchers receive pseudonymised records with classes smaller than five withheld. Test box mAP50 is 0.516 (potholes), 0.456 (litter) and 0.708 (graffiti), at 19.5 to 24.3 ms per photo on a laptop CPU.
